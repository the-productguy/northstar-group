FAQ

Franchising in Armenia, answered.

How does NorthStar Group help brands franchise in Armenia?

NorthStar Group acts as the master franchise and area development partner for Armenia - handling market research, capital investment, site selection, licensing, and local operations setup - so international brands can launch without building an in-house team first.

Does NorthStar Group fund the cost of opening a franchise in Armenia?

Yes. As a well-capitalized area development partner, NorthStar Group covers the investment required to launch, including real estate, buildout, and licensing fees, so brands don't need to raise capital themselves to enter the Armenian market.

Which industries is Armenia best suited for international franchises?

Food and beverage, retail, and hospitality brands see some of the strongest demand, driven by a young, urbanizing population and growing tourism, though NorthStar Group evaluates opportunities across categories.

How long does it take to bring a franchise to Armenia?

Timelines vary by brand and category, but our process typically moves through discovery, market research, investment structuring, site selection, and launch in a matter of months rather than years.

Is NorthStar Group affiliated with the Armenian government?

No. NorthStar Group is a privately owned investment and franchise advisory firm and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the Government of Armenia.

How do I start the process of franchising my brand in Armenia?

Contact NorthStar Group with a few details about your brand - we'll respond with an initial view on fit, opportunity, and next steps for entering the Armenian market.
