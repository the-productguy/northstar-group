FAQ

The estimator, explained.

How accurate is the Armenia Franchise Investment Estimator?

The estimator produces a planning-level range based on published Armenia market benchmarks (construction costs, wages, tax rates, import duties) and standard franchise industry fee structures. It is designed to size the opportunity directionally, not to replace a formal investment proposal, which NorthStar Group prepares after reviewing a brand's specific format and requirements.

Do I need to pay any of this investment myself?

No. As Armenia's master franchise and area development partner, NorthStar Group funds the investment shown by this estimator - real estate, buildout, equipment, licensing, and working capital - directly. Brands are not asked to fund their own market entry.

What is included in the estimate?

The estimate covers real estate fit-out and leasehold improvements, equipment and FF&E, lease deposit and advance rent, working capital and a ramp-up cash reserve, import duties and licensing, pre-opening costs, a contingency reserve, and master franchise or area development fees. It does not include ongoing royalties, which are set by the brand.

How is the master franchise or area development fee calculated?

The estimator applies a per-unit development fee for each location, plus a one-time master franchise or area development territory fee that reflects the value of exclusive national rights - consistent with how most international franchisors structure multi-unit agreements abroad.

Is VAT included in this estimate?

No. Armenia's 20% VAT is generally recoverable as an input tax credit for a VAT-registered business, so it is excluded from the capital estimate rather than double-counted as a cost. In practice it can still represent a temporary cash-flow timing item until reclaimed, which NorthStar Group manages as part of its financing.

Does this estimate assume NorthStar Group operates every location directly?

Yes. Figures assume NorthStar Group funds and operates each location directly, consistent with an area development structure. Under a master franchise structure where local sub-franchisees are recruited, some capital costs may be shared with those sub-franchisees rather than borne entirely by NorthStar Group.
