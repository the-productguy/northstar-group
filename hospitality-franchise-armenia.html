Hospitality Franchising

Hospitality franchise opportunities in Armenia.

Armenia's tourism sector is booming, and internationally branded hospitality concepts remain scarce. NorthStar Group funds and operates hospitality franchise launches across Armenia, from the first feasibility study to the grand opening.

The Opportunity

Tourism is growing faster than branded supply.

Armenia welcomed over 2.2 million international visitors in 2024, and that number continues to climb - but internationally branded hotel and hospitality concepts remain scarce relative to demand.

Most of Yerevan's hospitality supply is independently operated, leaving room for recognized hotel, serviced-apartment, and lifestyle hospitality brands to establish an early, defensible position in the market.

Why Hospitality Fits Armenia

The conditions international brands look for.

Visitor Growth Outpacing Branded Supply

Rising international arrivals are creating demand that Armenia's largely independent hospitality sector hasn't fully met.

Strategic, Accessible Location

Direct flight access across Europe, the Middle East, and Central Asia makes Yerevan an increasingly convenient stop for international travelers.

Diaspora & Business Travel

A globally networked Armenian diaspora and growing business-travel volume add a further, steady layer of demand beyond leisure tourism.

What NorthStar Group Handles

Fully funded, end to end.

Site Selection & Real Estate

Identifying and securing properties suited to the brand's hospitality format, from city-center hotels to serviced apartments.

Licensing & Regulatory Coordination

FAQ

Hospitality franchising in Armenia, answered.

Are international hotel brands currently operating in Armenia?

Representation is limited relative to visitor demand, leaving room for additional internationally branded hotel and hospitality concepts, particularly in Yerevan and near key tourist routes.

Does NorthStar Group fund the cost of opening a hospitality franchise in Armenia?

Yes. NorthStar Group funds real estate, buildout, and pre-opening costs required to launch, so brands don't need to raise capital themselves to enter Armenia.

What hospitality formats is NorthStar Group prioritizing in Armenia?

Branded hotels, serviced apartments, and lifestyle hospitality concepts see the strongest near-term fit, given Armenia's growing tourism and business-travel volume.

How long does it take to open a hospitality franchise in Armenia?

Timelines vary by format and whether the project involves new construction or renovation, but our process typically moves through discovery, market research, investment structuring, and buildout in a matter of months to just over a year for larger projects.
