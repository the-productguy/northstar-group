Why NorthStar

The criteria franchisors screen for - and where we stand.

Before signing a master franchise or area development partner, franchisors run candidates through a defined set of criteria. Here's how NorthStar Group measures up against each one, point by point.

The Standard

Franchisors don't take a partner on faith.

Master franchise and area development agreements hand one partner exclusive control over an entire territory - so franchisors screen candidates against a defined checklist before signing anything: financial capacity, operating experience, local market knowledge, real estate access, team capability, regulatory standing, and long-term commitment.

Rather than asking you to take our word for it, this page walks through that exact checklist - the standard, and where NorthStar Group stands against it. For more on the underlying framework, see the 3C standard, explained.

The Checklist

Eight criteria, measured honestly.

Financial Capacity

The standard: Sufficient capital - personal or institutional - to fund real estate, buildout, and working capital without relying on the brand for financing.

NorthStar Group: A privately funded investment group that commits its own capital to every launch - real estate, buildout, licensing, and working capital - with no capital call on the brand.

Operational & Business Experience

The standard: A demonstrated track record building, scaling, and operating businesses - not just capital on paper.

NorthStar Group: Founders who built and scaled GameBit, a B2B iGaming platform, and theConsultancy Partners, Armenia's leading digital transformation consultancy for banks and fintech - hands-on operating experience, not advisory distance.

Local Market Knowledge

The standard: Deep, on-the-ground understanding of consumer behavior, real estate, and regulatory conditions in the target market.

NorthStar Group: Full-time, Yerevan-based operators who've raised capital and built institutional relationships locally for over a decade - not a fly-in advisory team.

Real Estate & Site Development

The standard: Established relationships and capability to identify, negotiate, and secure the real estate a brand's format requires.

NorthStar Group: Site selection and real estate securement is a core service line, covering everything from flagship locations to multi-unit territory rollout.

Organizational & Team Capability

The standard: The ability to recruit, train, and manage a local team capable of operating to brand standard.

NorthStar Group: A track record of hiring and managing teams in Armenia through GameBit and theConsultancy Partners, plus a dedicated in-market team for legal, licensing, and hiring on each new brand launch.

Regulatory & Legal Standing

The standard: A clean legal and regulatory standing, with the ability to navigate local business registration, licensing, and compliance.

NorthStar Group: A privately owned, independently operated investment group with no government affiliation, coordinating regulatory and legal compliance directly with in-market partners for every launch.

Long-Term Territory Commitment

The standard: A multi-year commitment to developing the full territory, not a single opportunistic unit.

NorthStar Group: Structured for exclusive, long-term master franchise or area development agreements - our incentives are tied to territory-wide performance, not a single location.

Alignment & Accountability

The standard: A partner whose incentives are structurally aligned with the brand's success, with one accountable point of contact.

NorthStar Group: We fund the launch ourselves, so our success is permanently tied to the brand's - and we're the single accountable partner for the life of the agreement.

FAQ

The screening process, answered.

What criteria do franchisors use to select a master franchise or area development partner?

Most franchisors evaluate candidates against financial capacity, operational experience, local market knowledge, real estate access, team capability, regulatory standing, and long-term territory commitment - the same criteria outlined on this page.

Why does NorthStar Group fund the launch itself instead of asking brands to invest?

Self-funding is a core part of how NorthStar Group qualifies as a master franchise and area development partner - it removes fundraising risk from the brand and keeps our incentives aligned with the territory's long-term success.

Has NorthStar Group operated businesses in Armenia before?

Yes. NorthStar Group's founders built and scaled GameBit, a B2B iGaming platform, and theConsultancy Partners, a digital transformation consultancy for banks and fintech, both headquartered in Armenia.

How can a brand verify NorthStar Group meets its master franchise criteria?

Contact NorthStar Group directly - we're glad to walk any franchisor's due diligence team through our financials, team background, and territory plan in detail.
