Strategic Location

A bridge between Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.

Armenia sits at the crossroads of major trade routes, with membership in the Eurasian Economic Union giving preferential access to a market of roughly 180 million consumers, alongside deepening trade ties with the European Union.

Yerevan's growing international flight network and e-visa access for dozens of countries make it one of the more accessible capitals in the region for investors, executives, and expatriate talent.

Economic Fundamentals

A business environment built for growth.

Simple, Flat Taxation

A flat corporate tax rate and streamlined business registration make Armenia one of the easier markets in the region to set up and operate in.

The "Silicon Mountains"

A decade of sustained growth has turned Armenia's tech sector into a magnet for global capital — proof the market can absorb ambitious, world-class business models.

Free Economic Zones

Government-backed incentive zones offer preferential terms for qualifying investors, reducing the cost and friction of entry.

Talent

A trilingual, tech-fluent workforce.

Armenia's workforce is educated, digitally fluent, and typically operates across Armenian, Russian, and English — a rare combination that gives brands flexibility across customer-facing and back-office roles alike.

A global Armenian diaspora, deeply networked into business communities from Los Angeles to Dubai to Paris, adds a further layer of access and credibility for brands entering the market.

Sector Spotlight

Where global brands find the fastest fit.

Food & Beverage

A rapidly maturing dining culture with strong appetite for international quick-service and café concepts.

Retail

Rising disposable income and a young, brand-aware population driving demand for global retail names.

Technology

A proven ecosystem for technical talent, R&D operations, and regional hub functions.

Hospitality

A booming tourism sector creating demand for international hotel, leisure, and lifestyle brands.
