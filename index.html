Our Mission

Positioning Armenia as a globally competitive investment destination.

Our Vision

A recognized global business destination.

What We Do

One partner, fully funded, start to finish.

NorthStar Group doesn't just advise - as Armenia's master franchise and area development partner, we cover the investment required to bring your brand to Armenia, and manage every step of getting it open.

Franchising

End-to-end master franchise and area development structuring, licensing negotiation, and operational setup on the brand's behalf.

Investment

We fund the capital required to launch - real estate, buildout, licensing fees, and working capital.

Market Penetration

Market Research
