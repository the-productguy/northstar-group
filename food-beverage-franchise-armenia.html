Food & Beverage Franchising

Food & beverage franchise opportunities in Armenia.

Yerevan's dining culture is maturing fast. NorthStar Group is the master franchise and area development partner bringing international quick-service, cafe, and casual-dining brands into Armenia - fully funded, from site selection to grand opening.

The Opportunity

A dining culture that's outgrowing its options.

Yerevan's food and beverage scene has grown rapidly over the past decade, but international representation is still thin - most categories are served by local operators rather than the branded, standardized concepts consumers see when they travel.

A young, urbanizing population and a fast-growing tourism sector, which welcomed over 2.2 million international visitors in 2024, are creating steady demand for the quick-service, cafe, and casual-dining brands the world already trusts.

Why F&B Fits Armenia

The conditions international brands look for.

Underrepresented Categories

Fast-casual, specialty coffee, bakery-cafe, and casual-dining formats have little to no international competition in Armenia today.

A Young, Urban Consumer Base

Over a third of Armenia's population lives in Yerevan, where dining-out habits increasingly mirror those of European and Gulf capitals.

Tourism-Driven Demand

Rising visitor numbers support F&B concepts near hospitality and tourist corridors, extending demand beyond the local population alone.

What NorthStar Group Handles

Fully funded, end to end.

Site Selection

Identifying high-traffic locations that match brand format and unit economics, from flagship to express footprints.

Licensing & Supply Chain

FAQ

F&B franchising in Armenia, answered.

Which F&B categories is NorthStar Group prioritizing in Armenia?

Fast-casual, specialty coffee, bakery-cafe, and casual-dining concepts see the strongest near-term fit, though NorthStar Group evaluates F&B opportunities across formats.

What licensing is required to open a restaurant or cafe franchise in Armenia?

Food and beverage operators need food safety permits, standard business registration, and, depending on format, additional health and construction approvals. NorthStar Group coordinates this process directly with local partners and authorities.

Does NorthStar Group fund the cost of opening an F&B franchise in Armenia?

Yes. As a well-capitalized area development partner, NorthStar Group funds real estate, buildout, kitchen equipment, and working capital, so brands don't need to raise capital themselves to enter Armenia.

How is opening an F&B franchise in Armenia different from other markets?

The core process is similar to other markets - site selection, licensing, buildout, staffing - but Armenia's compact market lets NorthStar Group move from signed agreement to grand opening faster than in larger, more fragmented markets.
