The Structures

Master franchise vs. area development.

A master franchise agreement grants one partner exclusive rights to develop a brand across an entire country, including the ability to recruit and sub-license individual franchisees on the brand's behalf.

An area development agreement instead commits one partner to open and directly operate a set number of company-owned units within a defined territory, on a fixed timeline, without sub-licensing to other operators.

Both structures exist for the same reason: brands don't want to manage market entry unit by unit. They want one accountable, funded, local partner who owns the outcome for the entire territory. In Armenia, that partner is NorthStar Group.

FAQ

Master franchising and area development, answered.

What is the difference between a master franchise and an area development agreement?

A master franchise agreement grants one partner the exclusive right to develop a brand across an entire country or region, including the ability to sub-license individual units to other operators. An area development agreement instead commits one partner to open and directly operate a set number of units within a defined territory, without sub-licensing. NorthStar Group works with brands under either structure, depending on what fits their model and goals in Armenia.

Does NorthStar Group operate as a master franchisee or an area developer in Armenia?

Both, depending on the brand. Some partners prefer a single master franchisee who can sub-license across Armenia over time; others prefer an area developer who builds and runs company-operated locations directly. NorthStar Group structures the agreement to match the brand's long-term plan for the market.

What criteria does NorthStar Group use to evaluate a master franchise partnership?

NorthStar Group applies the same 3C standard most franchisors use to screen master franchise and area development candidates: well-capitalized, capable, and committed. We fund the launch ourselves, bring an operating team with a track record in Armenia, and take on exclusive, long-term territory rights so our incentives stay aligned with the brand.

Which industries is NorthStar Group targeting for master franchise and area development deals in Armenia?

Food and beverage, retail, and hospitality brands see the strongest near-term fit, driven by Armenia's young, urbanizing population and growing tourism sector, though NorthStar Group evaluates master franchise and area development opportunities across categories.

How does a brand start a master franchise or area development conversation with NorthStar Group?

Contact NorthStar Group with a few details about your brand and current international expansion plans. We'll respond with an initial view on fit, territory structure, and next steps for entering Armenia.
